The False Economy of "Dumb" Pipelines

Historically, organizations attempted to filter events using static regex strings in syslog daemons. However, regex engines that rely on Non-Deterministic Finite Automata (NFA) with backtracking suffer from Regular Expression Denial of Service (ReDoS). A maliciously crafted input forces exponential time complexity 𝒪(2N), causing CPUs to peg at 100% utilization.

Furthermore, pure regex rules cannot detect multi-step behavioral anomalies (such as a slow password-guessing attack across three valid attempts) and cannot explain novel or zero-day attack payloads.

The Need for Formal Language Theory

By formally classifying normal system telemetry as a Regular Language, we construct deterministic finite-state recognizers that guarantee linear time execution 𝒪(N) with mathematical certitude. The core problem of modern telemetry is not detection—it is rapid, zero-cost routing.

Our architecture routes 95% of routine entries directly to local storage at zero cost, reserving expensive AI inference exclusively for events that deviate from the formal language grammar.
