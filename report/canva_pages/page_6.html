The architectural foundation of our hybrid engine is anchored in the Chomsky Hierarchy of formal grammars. By mapping authentication telemetry parsing strictly to regular expressions and Type-3 automata, we achieve optimal computational bounds and eliminate algorithmic vulnerability.

Theorem 1: Immunity to ReDoS Attacks

Proposition: A compiled DFA with transition function δ: Q × Σ → Q guarantees exactly one transition per input character.
Proof: Since |δ(q, σ)| ≤ 1 for all states q ∈ Q and symbols σ ∈ Σ, the state traversal graph contains no non-deterministic branching. An adversary cannot trigger catastrophic backtracking O(2N), mathematically eliminating Regular Expression Denial of Service (ReDoS).

The Neuro-Symbolic Boundary Separation

Boundary Principle: Deterministic Chomsky Type-3 automata are used exclusively where mathematical determinism, zero memory growth, and microsecond guarantees are required. Type-0 neural models are invoked strictly downstream as heuristic oracles for semantic reasoning on the 4.8% anomaly tail.
